# Simulating macroeconomics with a web app

| Date | Organisation | Topic | Type |
| :----: | :------------: | :-----: | :----: |
| 2022 | UCL Economics | Macroeconomics/Education | Computational Methods |

Frustrated with the way I was being taught macroeconomics, I decided the most effective way to learn the content was to remake it all in python. I employed a lot of object oriented programming, trying to build simulation environments for me to introduce macroeconomic shocks and visualise the diagrams presented in lectures that never made sense to me.

After building a prototype, I realised how useful this would be to other people. In the macroeconomics exam at the end of the year, 50 students were using my web-app. I presented it to the UCL Economics department, then to a live audience at ExploreEcon 2022 where I won "Best Poster" for something that wasn't really a poster.
