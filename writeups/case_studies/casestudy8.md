# CitizenQuery-UK: A Person-Centric AI Benchmark

| Date | Organisation | Topic | Type |
| :----: | :------------: | :-----: | :----: |
| 2025/26 | Open Data Institute | LLM Benchmarking | Quantitative Research |

*   **Objective:** Designing a novel dataset and evaluation pipeline to measure LLM performance in citizen query tasks (misinformation dynamics).
*   **Key Work:** Led an international team on the design and development of the benchmark, exploring the dynamics of AI-generated misinformation.
*   **Impact:** Presented the bespoke evaluation method at **NeurIPS 2025**.
*   **Publication:** *The CitizenQuery Benchmark: A Novel Dataset and Evaluation Pipeline for Measuring LLM Performance in Citizen Query Tasks* (arXiv preprint).
