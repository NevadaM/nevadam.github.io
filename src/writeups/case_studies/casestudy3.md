# Planning optimal healthcare infrastructure in Africa

| Date | Organisation | Topic | Type |
| :----: | :------------: | :-----: | :----: |
| 2024 | Open Data Institute | Public Health | Geospatial Research |

In early 2024, I set myself the task of synthesising two open datasets into something with meaningful impact, and then communicating that in a more informal way to my typical work. The resulting article contained a surprisingly strong methodology and output, with my healthcare analysis finding its way to the World Bank transport team through a connection at [healthsites.io](healthsites.io). But something I'm more proud of is the way I pushed my authorial voice in this piece, speaking on some fine-grained technicalities with humour and lightness that culminated in a pretty successful reception.

You can read the piece on Medium [here](https://medium.com/odi-research/open-data-science-to-be-proud-of-geographic-healthcare-analysis-with-healthsites-io-and-worldpop-e33ee98d8a81). 

The code is entirely reusable, accessible on Github [here](https://github.com/NevadaM/geoconstrained-Voronoi/).
