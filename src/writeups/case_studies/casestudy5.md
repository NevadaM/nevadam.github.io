# Investigating the dirty world of fresher hazing at UCL

| Date | Organisation | Topic | Type |
| :----: | :------------: | :-----: | :----: |
| 2022/23 | The Cheese Grater (UCL) | Social Justice | Investigative Journalism |

At university, I wandered into The Cheese Grater at a time where I wanted to spend time doing something more than focusing on my degree in Economics. My stint at The Cheese Grater started from a desire to talk to more interesting people than my classmates in BSc Economics and ended with me seriously considering writing as a career. I worked on the investigative journalism side of the magazine (having been deemed not funny enough for the satire side) and, over a year and a half, built a role for myself as a dependable investigative writer with a frankly workaholic nature and distinct, characterful voice in my articles --- "sassy", as described by the only editor that liked it. 

I rose through the ranks and led several long-term pieces, culminating in a national award in 2023: "Best News Story" at the Student Publication Awards, given to my article that judges from Bloomberg commended for its methodical investigation and unique, sardonic voice.

The article illuminated a seedy underbelly of UCL sports society culture, with intiations supposedly banned but freshers instigated into volunteering for traumatic, sometimes permanent, harm. We exposed some harsh stuff, and somehow managed to uncover the finances underneath some of it. I led the team, and led the writing too, which gave me some crucial experience I've been able to lean on across my work ever since.

You can read the article [here](https://cheesegratermagazine.org/2022/11/18/chun-buckets-everywhere-how-initiation-ceremonies-ruin-the-freshers-experience/).
